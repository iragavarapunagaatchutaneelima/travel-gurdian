# Travel Guardian — Master Issue Inventory & Resolution Tracker

This document catalogs all identified architectural, navigation, routing, AI, emergency, UI/UX, and performance issues across the Travel Guardian platform, tracking their root causes, severity, status, and verified engineering solutions.

| **ISS-01** | G. GOOGLE MAPS / E. | **ISS-11** | G. | **ISS-12** | G. | **ISS-13** | J.
